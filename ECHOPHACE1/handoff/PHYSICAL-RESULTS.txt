ECHO-7 Part A: physical results checkpoint

Scope
Basic standalone Part A functionality at one indoor setup. Camera and marker
positions were user-reported fixed for the stationary batch; distance and
illumination were not measured. These are finite test observations, not full
Part A coverage across distance, angle, scale, lighting or scene complexity.
No marker/symbol identity, CNN decision, Arduino motion, closed-loop robot
behavior or full-robot R6 compliance is established.

Current profile
EPA2 / 78 result words, native 320 x 240 RGB565, ratio segmentation with
red minimum 64, blue minimum 32 and dominance 3/2. Causal neighbour cleanup;
4-connected row-reclaimed components; MIN_AREA 32; eight active handles and
eight completed candidate slots per colour. Overflow invalidates the whole
candidate frame. Candidate centres are integer bounding-box midpoints;
raw centroids are integer pixel-coordinate averages. They are different
point definitions even when they happen to give the same coordinates.

Stationary hands-free baseline
paired_v2_fixed_001 / 002 / 003: each CRC verified, 69/69 packet comparisons
match, one red and one blue valid candidate, no overflow. Main red centres
were (158,80) in all three; blue centres (158,147). Observed integer centre
ranges were zero pixels on both axes. Red areas 2101,2106,2114: mean 2107,
range 13, range/mean 0.617%. Blue areas 1912,1912,1917: mean 1913.667,
range 5, range/mean 0.261%. Red xmax changed from 184 to 185 despite the
unchanged floored centre. This is a three-sample single-condition baseline,
not subpixel stability or broad accuracy evidence.
Evidence: validation/fixed_batch_v2.json

Separated red distractor
red_single_v2_002: frame 17280, CRC 8629, 69/69 comparisons match. Valid
2 red / 1 blue, no overflow. Red cleaned areas 2097 and 301; blue 1925.
Global raw red centroid (167,93) versus the main raw component (157,81)
shows a same-frame, same-definition offset of (+10,+12) pixels. Components
remain separate; the extractor does not decide which component is the target.
Evidence: validation/red_separation_v2.json

Separated blue distractor

Blank scene
blank_v2_001: frame 23467, CRC ee14, 69/69 comparisons match. Valid empty
candidate frame, 0 red / 0 blue, no overflow. Raw red area 0; one isolated
raw blue pixel at (1,21), below MIN_AREA and removed by causal cleanup.
No coloured test targets or hands are visible. One blank scene does not
establish a general false-positive rate.
Evidence: validation/blank_scene_v2.json

Active-handle capacity and skin interference
paired_v2_handheld_001 / 002 / 003 all match numerically, but only 2/3 have
valid candidate frames and 0/3 have only one red and one blue. Holding fingers
produce extra red candidates of area 40 and 64 in the first two frames.
The third correctly becomes invalid on red active overflow: independent
prefix BFS peaks at nine handles. The first exceeding row is y49, with six
carried components plus three seeds in the visible upper-left hand/arm,
before the main printed red starts at y54. Both published counts are zero.
The active/output cause and handle peak are software diagnostics; the packet
transmits overflow flags, not separate peak counts. These handheld frames
are not fixed-scene repeatability evidence.
Evidence: validation/handheld_batch_v2.json

Completed-result capacity
red_distractor_v2_001: frame 538, CRC 2f50, 69/69 comparisons match. The main
red plus a nine-red grid produce ten qualifying red components, exceeding
the eight completed slots. Active red peak is only six. The whole candidate
frame is correctly invalid, both counts zero and every candidate valid bit
zero. Some invalid slot fields retain diagnostic values and must not be used
as detections. The grid card is near/crosses image edges, so this is a capacity
case rather than validation of every tile's physical geometry.
Evidence: validation/completed_capacity_v2.json

Retained fixture limitations and earlier captures
red_single_v2_001 remains a failed separation fixture attempt: the clipped,
dark tile at x301..319/y222..239 peaks at R57, below the R64 threshold. It
contributes zero red pixels even though 242/342 ROI pixels satisfy dominance.
Its numeric processing PASS is not a two-red scene PASS. The successful
retry was repositioned into the lit, visible part of the scene without a
threshold or source change.
Evidence: validation/red_single_framing_attempt_v2.json

paired_v2_001 was the first positive valid 1R1B frame, but the outer paper was
cropped and the blue component ended at y238. It is retained as limited
framing evidence, not substituted for the later hands-free batch.
Evidence: validation/first_positive_v2.json

Earlier EPA1/V1 captures and their reports are retained. V1 used a lifetime
provisional-label cap without row reclamation; fragmentation could overflow
even when the final image contained few large objects. Earlier threshold,
underlit, clipped and handheld observations remain in the evidence set.
They are not relabelled as successful scene tests or discarded from replay.
Replay and simulation records are separate from real measured packet IDs;
this text does not replace toolchain, timing or complete regression logs.

Verification basis
For the physical reviews above, raw transport/CRC and decoded pixels were
checked, colour masks and causal cleanup recomputed, and whole-image pixel
BFS checked measured component areas, bounds and centres. Capacity reviews
also independently used prefix BFS. All evidence JSON files identify the
raw dump SHA256 and distinguish measured words from software diagnostics.
Numeric agreement validates implemented processing of those pixels; physical
scene labels and correspondence were visually reviewed and remain limited
by the small number of conditions tested.
